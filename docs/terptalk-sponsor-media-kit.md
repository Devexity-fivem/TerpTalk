## About TerpTalk

TerpTalk is a pseudonymous, privacy-first online community for adult (21+)
cannabis cultivators. Members document real grows in structured diaries,
ask and answer cultivation questions, use a deterministic Plant Doctor
troubleshooting system, and contribute to a community-built strain
knowledge base.

What makes TerpTalk different:

- **Pseudonymous by design** — no email required, no real-name policy,
  no tracking pixels, no third-party analytics vendors.
- **Community-first** — forum, diaries, questions and live chat; not a
  marketplace and not a content farm.
- **Knowledge-compounding** — every grow, question, and harvest review
  feeds back into structured strain and technique knowledge for the next
  grower.
- **Reputation earned, never sold** — progression, badges and ranks are
  driven only by real contribution.

## Sponsorship Inventory Detail

### Sponsored Grow Lab
- **Sponsor gets:** labeled public grow diaries documenting real use of
  their equipment/genetics; a summary of outcomes (positive and
  negative); early credibility with a privacy-conscious grower audience.
- **Growers get:** equipment or genetics for the run, featured
  participation, normal community progression.
- **TerpTalk provides:** campaign structure, grower selection, labeling,
  and an outcome report.
- **Disclosure:** every sponsored diary and post is labeled.
- **Sponsor does NOT control:** what growers write, the outcomes
  reported, Plant Doctor conclusions, or moderation.

### Sponsored Knowledge Series
- Labeled educational series (e.g., "VPD fundamentals," "Drying and
  curing") authored by staff/community, sponsored by a brand.
- Content must be useful regardless of whether the reader buys anything.
- Editorial independence is absolute; the sponsor sees the label, not
  the red pen.

### Challenge Sponsorship
- "SCROG Challenge — powered by X." The sponsor names and funds the
  challenge/prizes.
- Winner selection, voting, reputation, and moderation remain entirely
  community/system-controlled.

### Creator Campaign
- A verified grow-content creator runs a TerpTalk grow diary as a
  companion to their channel, with a labeled sponsor.
- Loop: video → diary → community discussion → harvest → knowledge.

### Affiliate Partnership
- Product listing + tracked outbound links on `/deals`, promo codes,
  category placement, rank-gated "Garden Perks" offers available.
- Commission-based; already live and serving.

## What We Will Never Sell

- Member data — individual, behavioral, or private. Ever.
- Private diaries, private Plant Doctor cases, messages, notifications.
- Answer ranking, search ranking, grow matching, or strain statistics.
- Reputation, progression, badges, or moderation outcomes.
- The right to suppress a negative outcome or an honest review.

**Sponsors pay for exposure and documentation — never for praise,
placement in answers, or influence over community systems.**

## Disclosure Policy

All commercial content is labeled clearly and conspicuously, consistent
with FTC 16 CFR Part 255 (clear-and-conspicuous, unavoidable disclosure
of material connections). Affiliate links carry a written disclosure and
`rel="sponsored nofollow"`. Sponsored diaries, guides, challenges, and
creator campaigns carry explicit labels at the point of content.

they're founding relationships with a grower community being built to
